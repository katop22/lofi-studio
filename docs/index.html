The Pipeline

From Prompt to Published in Minutes

Lo-Fi Studio handles the entire creative and distribution pipeline end-to-end.

Generate Music

Describe the vibe you want and Suno AI produces a full lo-fi track — instrumental, high quality, ready for distribution.

Create Artwork

Stable Diffusion generates atmospheric scene artwork — cozy rooms, rain-swept windows, moonlit desks — matched to the music mood.

Render Video

FFmpeg combines the audio and artwork into a 16:9 video with gentle Ken Burns motion — optimized for streaming platforms.

Generate Captions

Ollama writes platform-specific captions, hashtags, and descriptions — evocative, on-brand, and SEO-friendly for each channel.

Distribute Everywhere

One command ships the track to YouTube, Pinterest, X, and more — with idempotent write-back so re-runs never double-post.

Publish Where Your Audience Lives

Lo-Fi Studio posts to multiple platforms in a single run, adapting content format and captions to each channel's best practices.
